# Dookie TV — One-Shot Continuous Animation Prompt Framework (Segmind Seedance 2.0)

This framework turns a topic and mascot brief into a ready-to-paste one-shot continuous animation prompt — a single smooth 10-second shot with precise camera direction, seamless event timing, audio synchronization, and visual styling for Segmind Seedance 2.0.

**Hard Rule: The final prompt MUST be 1,500 characters or fewer.**

---

## 1. Mascot Visual Anchors (Fixed 3D Pixar Style)

Every prompt must reference `image 1 and image 2` for mascot visual binding:
- **Dookie**: `Dookie, the boxer puppy mascot character from image 1 and image 2...`
- **Mia**: `Mia, the white kitten mascot character from image 1 and image 2...`
- **Carrot**: `Carrot, the orange bunny mascot character from image 1 and image 2...`

**Styling Directives**:
- 3D Pixar animated cartoon style, stylized cute 3D character, smooth 3D render.
- Palette: Sunshine Yellow `#FFD166` (60% background), Sky Blue `#4EA8DE` (30% secondary), Bubblegum Pink `#EF476F` (10% action/pops).
- NO photo-realistic animals, NO live-action, NO 2D flat drawings.

---

## 2. One-Shot Continuous Sequence Rules (10.0 Seconds Total)

- **Total Duration**: EXACTLY 10.0 seconds.
- **Single Continuous Shot**: NO camera cut scenes, NO multi-shots, NO abrupt transitions. The entire video is ONE smooth continuous shot.
- **Camera Parameters**: Specify 4 camera parameters at the very beginning of the prompt:
  1. **Shot Size**: Wide, Medium, Close-up.
  2. **Lens Equivalent**: 24mm, 35mm, 50mm.
  3. **Camera Angle**: Low angle, High angle, Eye-level.
  4. **Camera Movement**: Slow push in, Pull out, Track, Orbit, Static.

---

## 3. Audio & Synchronization Rules

- **Spoken Narration**: Spoken ONLY by an off-screen friendly child narrator voice.
- **Dialogue Timing**: Spoken dialogue lines placed inside speech marks at exact timestamps (e.g. `At 00:02, off-screen child voice narrates "Red!"`).
- **Visual-Audio Sync**: Explicitly describe visual pop/action sync (e.g. `At 00:02, a glowing red ball pops up beside him as off-screen child voice narrates "Red!"`).
- **Mascot SFX & Mouth Movement Sync**: Mascots NEVER speak human words. Whenever the mascot makes an animal sound effect (woof/bark/meow/squeak), the prompt MUST explicitly instruct the mouth opening action at that exact timestamp (e.g., `At 00:03, Dookie opens his mouth wide and barks happy puppy woofs, mouth movement and barking sound synchronized frame-by-frame`).
- **NO AI Text on Screen**: Must explicitly state: `NO on-screen text, NO subtitles, NO captions generated by AI. Clean 3D video frames only.`

---

## 4. Metadata Footer (Mandatory Block)

Every prompt ends with these two metadata lines:
```text
Location: [Location description], [Time of day].
Audio: Off-screen friendly child narrator voice matching vocal_reference.mp3, upbeat cheerful children's background music matching music_reference.mp3, mascot species-specific SFX only (NO mascot human speech).
```

---

## 5. Example Output Format

```text
Single continuous one-shot video clip, Medium 35mm lens, eye-level, slow push in — Dookie, the boxer puppy mascot from image 1 and image 2, sits happily in a bright playroom with a sunshine yellow background (#FFD166). At 00:02, a glowing 3D red ball pops up beside him, off-screen child voice narrates "Red!". At 00:05, a glowing 3D blue ball pops up, off-screen child voice narrates "Blue!". At 00:08, a glowing 3D pink ball pops up, off-screen child voice narrates "Pink!". Dookie opens his mouth wide at 00:09 and barks happy puppy woofs, mouth movement and barking sound synchronized frame-by-frame. NO on-screen text, NO subtitles, NO AI captions.

Location: Bright colorful playroom, sunny morning.
Audio: Off-screen friendly child narrator voice matching vocal_reference.mp3, upbeat cheerful children's background music matching music_reference.mp3, mascot puppy barks (NO mascot human speech).
```
