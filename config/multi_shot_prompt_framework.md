# Dookie TV — Multi-Shot Animation Prompt Framework (Segmind Seedance 2.0)

This framework turns a topic and mascot brief into a ready-to-paste multi-shot animation prompt — a timecoded sequence of 2 to 4 shots (12 to 15 seconds total) with precise camera direction, audio synchronization, and visual styling for Segmind Seedance 2.0.

**Hard Rule: The final prompt MUST be 1,500 characters or fewer.**

---

## 1. Mascot Visual Anchors (Fixed 3D Pixar Style)

Every prompt must reference `image 1 and image 2` for mascot visual binding:
- **Dookie**: `Dookie, the boxer puppy mascot character from image 1 and image 2...`
- **Mia**: `Mia, the white kitten mascot character from image 1 and image 2...`
- **Carrot**: `Carrot, the orange bunny mascot character from image 1 and image 2...`

**Styling Directives**:
- 3D Pixar animated cartoon style, stylized cute 3D character, smooth 3D render.
- Palette: Sunshine Yellow `#FFD166` (60% background), Sky Blue `#4EA8DE` (30% secondary), Bubblegum Pink `#EF476F` (10% action/pops).
- NO photo-realistic animals, NO live-action, NO 2D flat drawings.

---

## 2. Multi-Shot Sequence Rules (12–15 Seconds Total)

- **Total Duration**: 12.0 to 15.0 seconds.
- **Shot Count**: 2 to 4 shots per sequence (each shot between 3 to 5 seconds).
- **Timecode Format**: Each shot begins with `[MM:SS - MM:SS]`.
- **Camera Parameters Per Shot**:
  1. **Shot Size**: Wide, Medium, Close-up, Macro.
  2. **Lens Equivalent**: 24mm, 35mm, 50mm, 85mm.
  3. **Camera Angle**: Low angle, High angle, Eye-level, Over-the-shoulder.
  4. **Camera Movement**: Push in, Pull out, Track, Orbit, Pan, Tilt, Static.

---

## 3. Audio & Synchronization Rules

- **Spoken Narration**: Spoken ONLY by an off-screen friendly child narrator voice.
- **Dialogue Timing**: Spoken dialogue lines placed inside speech marks within the exact timecoded shot where it lands (e.g. `off-screen child voice narrates "Look! One red ball!"`).
- **Visual-Audio Sync**: Explicitly describe visual pop/action sync (e.g. `As number 1 pops up on screen, off-screen child voice narrates "One!"`).
- **Mascot SFX & Mouth Movement Sync**: Mascots NEVER speak human words. Whenever the mascot makes an animal sound effect (woof/bark/meow/squeak), the prompt MUST explicitly instruct the mouth opening action at that exact timestamp (e.g., `At 00:03, Dookie opens his mouth wide and barks happy puppy woofs, mouth movement and barking sound synchronized frame-by-frame`).
- **NO AI Text on Screen**: Must explicitly state: `NO on-screen text, NO subtitles, NO captions generated by AI. Clean 3D video frames only.`

---

## 4. Metadata Footer (Mandatory Block)

Every prompt ends with these two metadata lines:
```text
Location: [Location description], [Time of day].
Audio: Off-screen friendly child narrator voice matching vocal_reference.mp3, upbeat cheerful children's background music matching music_reference.mp3, mascot species-specific SFX only (NO mascot human speech).
```

---

## 5. Example Output Format

```text
[00:00 - 00:05] Wide, 24mm, eye-level, track — Dookie, the boxer puppy mascot from image 1 and image 2, sits happily in a bright playroom with sunshine yellow background (#FFD166). As a glowing 3D number 1 pops up beside him, off-screen child voice narrates "Look! Dookie counts one!". Dookie winks and barks with joy.

[00:05 - 00:10] Medium, 50mm, high angle, push in — A glowing sky blue 3D number 2 pops up next, off-screen child voice narrates "And number two!". Dookie tilts his head playfully and waves his paw.

[00:10 - 00:15] Close-up, 85mm, low angle, static — Dookie smiles brightly as both 3D numbers bounce rhythmically around him. NO on-screen text, NO subtitles, NO AI captions.

Location: Bright colorful playroom, sunny morning.
Audio: Off-screen friendly child narrator voice, upbeat cheerful children's background music, mascot puppy barks (NO mascot human speech).
```
